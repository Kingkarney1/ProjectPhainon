OPERATION PHAINON: EARTH TO DEEP SPACE
Private playtest build 0.1.0

Thank you for testing. This is an early prototype of a spacecraft cockpit simulator: you sit in
the command seat, power the spacecraft up switch by switch, and fly it from the launch pad into
orbit. One mission, about ten minutes. Please do not share this build.


WHAT YOU NEED
  - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or a Mac running macOS 12 or later, or 64-bit Linux
  - A graphics card or integrated graphics from roughly 2017 or later, with up-to-date drivers
  - A mouse with a scroll wheel (a laptop trackpad works, but is awkward)
  - About 300 MB of free disk space
  - Sound on: the alarms and engine tell you a lot


INSTALLING ON WINDOWS
  1. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All". Do not run the game from inside the zip.
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click OperationPhainon.exe.
  3. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the build is not code-signed.
     Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  Nothing is installed on your system. To remove the game, delete the folder.
  Keep all the files in the folder together; the .exe does not work on its own.


INSTALLING ON A MAC
  1. Double-click the .tar.gz file to unpack it. You get an "OperationPhainon" folder.
  2. This test build is not signed by Apple, so macOS will refuse to open it until you clear the
     download flag. Open the Terminal app, type the following with a space at the end:
         xattr -cr
     then drag OperationPhainon.app from the folder into the Terminal window and press Return.
  3. Double-click OperationPhainon.app.
     If macOS still blocks it: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
     "Open Anyway".
  On a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) the game runs through Rosetta. macOS offers to install
  Rosetta the first time; accept it.
  To remove the game, delete the folder.


INSTALLING ON LINUX
  Unpack the .tar.gz and run OperationPhainon.x86_64 from the unpacked folder.


CONTROLS
  Look around ............ hold RIGHT mouse button and move the mouse
  Jump to a view ......... 1 window, 2 displays, 3 main panel, 4 left console, 5 right console,
                           6 overhead panel
  Zoom ................... hold Z (or the middle mouse button)
  Press / flip ........... LEFT click a button or switch
  Guarded buttons ........ the ones in a red frame: first click lifts the guard, second click presses
  Turn a dial ............ hold left click and drag, or point at it and scroll
  Throttle ............... Shift up, Ctrl down, T full, X idle (or drag the lever, left console)
  Fly by hand ............ W S pitch, A D yaw, Q E roll (needs GUIDANCE off and RCS on)
  Outside cameras ........ CAM SELECT on the overhead panel (key 6) swaps the right-hand display
                           between the hull camera and a chase camera. Watch a launch on chase.
  Hide / show the guide .. H
  Pause, restart, quit ... Esc

  The box at the top left always says what to do next, and the control it wants pulses amber.
  The displays are small from the seat: press 2 and hold Z to read them.


FLYING THE MISSION
  1. Overhead panel (press 6): switch BATT A, BATT B and APU on.
  2. Main panel (press 3): press MAIN POWER. The displays boot.
     Switch FUEL PUMP, TANK PRESS and S-BAND XMIT on. Press NAV SYSTEM, COMMS and CABIN SYS.
  3. Right console (press 5): turn NAV MODE to ASCENT and switch GUIDANCE on.
  4. The centre display lists every system as GO or NO-GO. When all are GO, press LAUNCH SEQ.
  5. Press T for full throttle. At T-6 go to the left console (press 4), lift the guard on
     ENGINE IGNITION and press it. You lift off at T-0.
  6. On the way up, ease the throttle back if a red warning appears (too much air pressure or G).
     MASTER ALARM on the main panel silences the alarm.
  7. When the booster burns out: lift the guard and press STAGE SEP, then ENGINE IGNITION again.
  8. When ORBIT GO lights up, press ENGINE CUTOFF. You are in orbit.

  You can fail: running out of fuel, falling back to Earth, or pressing ABORT ends the mission.
  Esc, then "Restart mission" starts again.


KNOWN ISSUES
  - Placeholder art: the cockpit is built from simple shapes, and all sound is synthesised.
  - No settings menu yet: no volume, resolution, graphics or key-binding options. Alt+Enter
    (Windows, Linux) or Cmd+F (Mac) switches between full screen and a window.
  - No save. The mission is short enough to replay.
  - No gamepad support.


WHAT WE WOULD LIKE TO HEAR
  - Where did you get stuck or confused, and for how long?
  - Could you find and read what you needed on the three displays?
  - Did operating the buttons, switches, dials and throttle feel right?
  - Did it reach orbit for you? If not, what ended the mission?
  - Any crash, freeze, black or pink screen, or missing sound. Please include your graphics card.
  - What did you most want to do that the game would not let you?

  If something goes wrong, the log file helps a great deal. Please attach Player.log from:
    Windows   %USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Phainon Flight Systems\Operation Phainon
              (paste that line into the File Explorer address bar)
    Mac       ~/Library/Logs/Phainon Flight Systems/Operation Phainon
              (in Finder: Go menu, Go to Folder, paste that line)
    Linux     ~/.config/unity3d/Phainon Flight Systems/Operation Phainon
